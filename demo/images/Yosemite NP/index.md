# Yosemite National Park

Three days in the valley, 11–13 June 2015.

<!-- @pg-date 2015-06-11 -->
## Arrival

We reached the park in the afternoon and stayed out until the evening light on the granite walls.

<!-- @pg-date 2015-06-12 -->
## The long hike

An early start from the valley floor, up past the waterfalls, and back in the evening:
about twelve hours on the trail. The GPS track is in this folder, so the map shows the whole route.

> **Alvin's diary, day 2.** They left at dawn and came back in the dark, limping. Twelve hours to climb one hill
> and come back down. I did it in twenty minutes, then had a nap in the sun on the car roof.

<!-- @pg-date 2015-06-13 -->
## Leaving the valley

One last stop before heading north to Lake Tahoe.
