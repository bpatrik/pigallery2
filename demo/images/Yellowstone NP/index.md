# Yellowstone National Park

The world's first national park, and more than half of the world's geysers. 20–22 June 2015.

## Mammoth Hot Springs

The travertine terraces of Mammoth change colour with every step.

## Geyser basins

One more morning at Mammoth, then a cloudy afternoon along the Firehole River, among bubbling pools and steaming vents.
